# Curriculum research and mapping

## What was checked

The chapter order comes from the table of contents of Allen P. Ross, *Introducing Biblical Hebrew* (Baker Academic, 2001), in the publisher-authorized [Biblia preview](https://biblia.com/api/plugins/embeddedpreview?resourceName=LLS%3AINTROBIBHBRW). The preview shows contents only and stops after chapter 37. No lesson body, vocabulary list, exercise or answer key was read or copied. The publisher offers no student eSources and limits the key to verified professors, and that key wasn't accessed.

Anything past chapter 37 is unverified. Chapter placement for hollow and geminate verbs is unverified. The per-lesson vocabulary, exact Genesis assignments and Ross's own mastery standards are unknown.

## Verified order, in brief

Chapters 1 to 6 are signs and sounds: alphabet; vowels and syllables; shewa and inseparable prepositions; accents, meteg, maqqef and qamets-hatup; dagesh, mappiq, the article and the Tetragrammaton; quiescent and guttural letters. Chapters 7 to 9 are nouns, sentences, the conjunction, noun patterns, min and adjectives. **The first verb comes at 10, before pronouns (11) and the construct (12).** Chapters 13 to 15 cover prepositional suffixes, existence, III-Aleph, the relative and noun suffixes. Chapters 16 to 25 cover the participle, imperfect, narrative sequence (18), statives, volitives, infinitives, verbal suffixes and numbers. Chapters 26 to 31 are the Niphal, Piel, Pual, Hithpael, Hiphil and Hophal. Chapters 32 to 37 are the weak verb groups.

## Vocabulary choices

Ten real Hebrew lexemes, each with its citation form from Strong and an attested Genesis form or line checked in `data/attestations.json`: אָב, אָח, אִישׁ, אֵם, אֶרֶץ, אִשָּׁה, בֵּן, בַּת, מֶלֶךְ, סֵפֶר. The two added beyond the initial eight, אֶרֶץ and מֶלֶךְ, bring final tsade and final kaf into capture. Final nun and final mem already arrive through בֵּן and אֵם. Final pe appears only in lessons and the trial.

Roots are null unless Strong gives an explicit derivation that was checked by hand (מֶלֶךְ from H4427, סֵפֶר from H5608). Nothing is derived by removing points. Keep lemma, attested form and root distinct: אִשָּׁה is listed under its own entry and not as אִישׁ plus an ending.

Sourced isn't the same as approved. A qualified Biblical Hebrew reviewer has not checked these glosses, transliterations or distractors.
